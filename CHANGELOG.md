# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [Unreleased]

### Security

- The sign-in page no longer shows the code it emailed ("Email from VirtualBiz · Your code is … · Fill in") on a developer's machine either: codes are only read from the email, as everywhere else.
- Sign-in codes and invite links are no longer handed out on the public demo: they were in the API reply (and on screen), so anyone could sign in to or reset any account there. Outside a developer's machine and the tests they now go by email only, and the API refuses to start if they're exposed (docs/ops/security.md). Phone sign-in is hidden, and refused by the API, while no SMS provider is connected. Logs no longer carry codes or full email addresses.

### Fixed

- The profile switcher no longer says "You have no businesses yet." to someone with no shop; it just offers to create one.
- Some dialogs (invite someone, add / edit customer, console staff, categories and promo codes, opening hours) greyed out only the panel they were opened from, leaving the sidebar and header bright and clickable: a full-screen backdrop drawn inside a blurred panel is trapped in that panel. They now open at the top of the workspace (a shared `Portal`), so the backdrop covers the screen.
- Shop promotions stopped at the start of their end date (a 1–31 October deal showed "Ended" all of 31 October). They now run through the whole end date and end by themselves at midnight; both promotion forms say so.
- "View in marketplace" from the console, and any link to a shop that hasn't listed anything yet, showed "Shop not found": the shop page only looked in the list of shops with listings. It now asks for the shop itself; only unknown, suspended or rejected shops are "not found", and the console disables the link for those with the reason.
- The marketplace categories wrap onto a second row no more: they scroll sideways in one row (swipe, or arrows with a mouse).
- Filter dropdowns were announced with every option in their name ("Status Any New Delivered …"); they now read as their label.
- Product shop settings changed on another device or by another team member now show up when the workspace opens (they were kept from the browser's saved copy).
- Team members with no colour (the owner added when a shop opens) showed as an empty ring in the provider workspace. They now get a steady colour from the team palette.
- The provider's online requests no longer promise clients an SMS; they hear back in their VirtualBiz inbox.
- Signed out while moving between pages: a sign-in renewal cut off by navigation (or lost to a network blip or a server error) was treated as a rejected session and cleared it. Only a 401 / 403 from the server signs the user out now.
- Seller forms: a field next to one showing an error no longer stretches and drops out of line. Adjust stock's quantity has −/+ and quick amounts (+1, +5, +10, +50) and takes digits only.
- Create Business: a second shop could not be created, and another account on the same browser could not create one. The wizard's draft was shared by everyone on the browser and stayed on the "open" screen after a shop opened (and kept that shop's id, so the next "Open my shop" created nothing). The draft now belongs to the account that started it, and a finished draft starts a new shop unless the done screen was just reached (e2e "One account, many shops").

### Added

- Homepage banners can have a background photo: Settings → Homepage → Background photo (upload, change, remove), shaded so the text stays readable, with the preview. On the public demo uploaded photos now live in the database (`vbiz.storage.type: db`), so they no longer vanish when Render restarts the server.
- Two-step sign-in for every account, not only console staff: turn it on in Your account → Settings with an authenticator app (QR code or key, then 10 recovery codes to save). Every sign-in (password, email code, Google, phone, password reset) then asks for a code from the app, or a recovery code; five wrong codes mean signing in again. Turning it on or off is confirmed by email (docs/api/two-step.md).
- Notification choices per topic: orders, bookings and, for shop owners, new orders and bookings, low stock, reviews, and ID check and payouts, each by email and by push (it was one switch for all emails). The inbox still gets everything.
- Terms of service and Privacy policy pages (`/terms`, `/privacy`) in English and Khmer, with a starting text. The footer links and the sign-up form's links went nowhere before; the sign-up form opens them in a new tab, so a half-typed sign-up is kept.
- The console can edit the marketplace homepage's banners (Settings → Homepage: each banner's text in English and Khmer, where it leads, shown or hidden, with a live preview) and the Terms and Privacy text (Settings → Terms & privacy). Super-admins change them; every change is in the audit log (docs/api/site-content.md).
- Appearance in Your account → Settings: light, dark, or follow the device, remembered on that device and applied before the page draws.
- Shop and salon settings are in sections (Shop profile, Location and hours, Orders and stock or Booking rules, Delivery, Payments and payouts, Receipts and tax, Export data) with links to Team, Promotions or Marketing, Notifications and Your account. On a phone, settings is a list and each section opens on its own with a way back, instead of one 7,000-pixel page; on a computer the sections sit in a menu beside the open one. Each section has its own link (`/seller/settings?section=payments`), which the "fix your ID check" banner now uses.
- A VirtualBiz logo and app icon: a shop awning over a "V" on the yellow tile, replacing the plain "B" everywhere it showed (marketplace, landing page, sign-in, invites, admin console). The browser tab, iPhone home screen, installed web app (new manifest) and push notifications use it too (docs/ui/brand.md).
- Log in and sign up: with Firebase, "Continue with Google" opens Google's account chooser straight away (no extra "Choose an account" step). The card, its buttons and fields have clear borders, hover and focus states, in light and dark.
- Redesigned service page, in the product page's style: the photos beside the title, price (with KHR) and quick facts (duration, professionals, free cancellation), then a full-width booking area with numbered steps (professional, date, time grouped by morning / afternoon / evening) and a sticky "Your booking" summary. On phones, Continue sits in a bar at the bottom.
- Firebase sign-in and push notifications (docs/ops/firebase.md). With the Firebase web config set, Google sign-in uses Google's account chooser and email + password is checked by Firebase; accounts made before Firebase move over the first time they sign in, with the same password and lockout. VirtualBiz keeps its own sessions and roles. My settings has a "Push notifications on this device" switch: every inbox notification then also pops up on that browser (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and signing out stops it. Without the config, sign-in works as before.
- Redesigned product page: the shop's real photos (thumbnails, arrows, full screen; a swipeable strip with dots on phones; product cards across the marketplace now show the cover photo too), a buy box with the price, the shop's live promotions for the item, options with sold-out ones marked and the stock of the options chosen (it showed the product's total, so sold-out colours looked available), share and save, delivery and the shop; then section links, a details card with a specifications table, reviews, more from the shop and similar items from other shops. On phones the Add to cart / Buy now bar stays at the bottom in place of the tab bar.
- Every notification is also emailed (docs/ops/email.md § Notification emails): customers about their orders and bookings (now including "order placed", also new in their inbox), shop owners and managers about new orders, booking requests, cancellations, reviews, verification, payouts and category decisions; English then Khmer, with a button to the right page, sent in the background once the change is saved. My settings has a switch to turn them off. Gmail can be the sender (an app password in `backend/.env`).

Locally they go to Mailpit (http://localhost:8025; also in the Docker setup). They're sent only once the change is saved, and a mail server that is down never undoes an invite.

Decline asks once more, closes the invite and shows in the shop's activity log ("Declined an invite"; joining shows "Joined the team"). Both buttons open the invite page rather than acting directly, so mail scanners that open links can't accept or decline.
- Accounts without a password (made with an email code or Google): signing in with a password now says so and offers **Email me a code** or **Set a password**, instead of "wrong password" (and no longer counts towards the lockout); My settings can set a first password.

Arrow keys and Enter pick one, and its chip shows the name. Other people's accounts are never listed from part of an address.
- Every list pages, 10 rows by default (docs/api/phase15-lists.md § Every list pages): the seller's open order tabs (they silently stopped at 60), products, inventory, promotions and reviews; the provider's services, retail products, deals and reviews; the console's category suggestions (only the first 50 showed); the customer's orders, bookings and saved items, with page numbers and a rows-per-page choice kept in the URL. The marketplace's shop pages, reviews and deals show 12 and Show more. Home-page queues show 5 and "See all"; the account sidebar and the workspace switcher no longer list every shop at once (Show all / a search). The default page size is 10 everywhere, the API's included.

Changing a filter keeps the old results faded under "Updating results…" until the new ones arrive. The search API pages its results (`page`, `size`) and returns the totals; it used to send every match at once.
- Invite the team by email alone, several at once (docs/api/phase10-shop-team.md): the invite dialog takes up to 20 addresses (typed, or pasted as a list; each becomes a chip, typos show in red) and one role, sends each separately and lists the ones it couldn't send with why (already on the team, not an email). There's no name to fill in: each person gives their own name when they join, and the join page now asks for it instead of using the start of their email.
- Sidebar counts explain themselves (docs/ui/navigation.md): hovering a count says what it counts ("18 orders are stuck: not accepted within 30 minutes, or not sent out within a day") and clicking it opens the list filtered to exactly those rows, so the table matches the number. The console's orders gain a **Stuck** tab (`status=stuck`, the same rule as the overview's queue) with a line saying what it means; the provider calendar opens its booking requests from `?requests=1` and marketing its reviews from `?tab=reviews`.
- New navigation for the workspaces and the console (docs/ui/navigation.md): a sidebar with named, grouped pages instead of the narrow icon rail, with the shop (or console) at the top, counts for waiting work, Settings, Explore (the marketplace, in a new tab) and Log out at the bottom for every role. A round handle half-way down its edge collapses it to icons (remembered on the device; automatic on tablets). On phones a bottom bar has the main pages, and More opens every page in a drawer (Promotions, Reviews, Team and others used to be unreachable from the phone's bar). The header puts search on the left and the actions on the right; Activity log is now in the sidebar.
- One date picker everywhere a date is chosen (docs/api/phase15-lists.md, "Date picker"): in the console and both workspaces, list date filters, the export period, promotion and deal dates, the calendar's Go to date, appointment and blocked-time days, closure days and the register's receipts day open the same calendar: quick ranges, two months where the first click is the start and the second the end, Apply / Cancel, keyboard navigation, Khmer month names, and a bottom sheet on phones. It replaces the browser's own date boxes and the "Custom range…" pair of inputs; filter chips show dates as "1 Oct – 21 Oct 2026" instead of ISO dates.
- Category suggestions (docs/api/phase19-category-requests.md): shops pick their marketplace category in Settings and, when none fits, suggest one; super-admins approve it as a new category or map it to an existing one (moving the shop), or decline with a reason the shop reads, from a queue on Settings → Categories and the overview. Every step is audited and the shop is told in its inbox.
- Shop activity log (docs/api/phase18-shop-activity.md): owners and managers see who did what in their shop (team invites and role changes, settings, products, services, rejected and returned orders, refunds, customers, promotions, imports and exports), filtered by person, area, action and date, with saved views and CSV export, from a new Activity log page linked on the Team page. Entries are written in the same transaction as the action, so refused actions leave none.
- More lists (docs/api/phase17-more-lists.md): inventory's Stock history pages through every stock change with in/out, reason, product and date filters and a CSV (it showed at most 40); a Notifications page in both workspaces holds the whole inbox with an unread filter, linked from the bell; service categories turn online booking on or off for all their services at once; calendar-only team members can be removed and restored; and every main list can save its filters as named views kept on the person's account.
- History lists (docs/api/phase16-shop-lists.md): the register's Receipts finds any receipt by number or customer on any day, so old sales can be refunded; the provider Sales table pages through every sale (it showed the latest 40); the provider calendar gains a List view of every appointment with status, staff, service, source and date filters and export, loads days outside its start-up window when you go there (they looked empty), and accepts several booking requests at once (selected in the list, or every request without a clash from the requests drawer). Seller reviews filter by waiting-for-reply and stars, promotions by status, with search.
- Shop lists, imports and exports (docs/api/phase16-shop-lists.md): product shops reach every order (done, cancelled and all orders page on the server with search, fulfilment, payment, source and date filters, sorting and an export of exactly that view; before, only the latest 60 could be seen). Customers in both workspaces page on the server with lifetime orders or visits and spend, VIP / new / at-risk segments worked out over all history, filters in the URL, Add customer and CSV import. Products import from CSV (update price, cost and counted stock by SKU, or add new products), with a dry run showing each row's problems first, and can be published, moved to draft or deleted in bulk.
- Management lists (docs/api/phase15-lists.md): one shared table, paginator and filter bar for every list. In the super-admin console every list now sorts by its columns on the server, filters by date (presets or custom days) and new fields (shop category and district, order fulfilment and payment, replied reviews, audit action), pages with page numbers and a rows-per-page choice, keeps all of it in the URL, and exports exactly what it shows as CSV (audited). Shops, accounts and reviews can be selected and verified, rejected, suspended, reinstated, signed out, hidden or shown in bulk, each row audited and refused rows reported. Promo codes and payout balances page and search too, and the payout totals no longer depend on what's loaded.
- Customer records and exports (docs/api/phase14-customers-exports.md): shops edit a customer's name, phone, email, district and private note from the customer drawer (product shops could not edit customers at all before). **Settings → Export data** downloads orders, sales, products, bookings and customers as CSV for a chosen period, ready for Excel in Khmer and safe from spreadsheet formulas; owners and managers only, and every export is logged.
- Messages that really go out (docs/api/phase13-messages.md): receipts from the register and the service checkout are shared as a private link (Telegram, SMS, copy, print) that opens a public receipt page; the product invoice's **Email invoice** opens the customer's mail app with the order filled in. A salon's decline message is saved and shown to the customer in their inbox and on the booking page. Booked clients get one reminder before their visit at the shop's reminder hours (inbox notification, plus an SMS through a logging stub until a provider is contracted).
- Catalog structure (docs/api/phase12-catalog-structure.md): product shops manage their collections (add, rename, reorder, delete choosing where the products go) from Products → Collections, and a new shop starts with collections for its category instead of electronics for everyone. Service providers manage their service categories (add, rename with services following, reorder, remove unused) instead of a fixed Hair / Nails / Make-up list, and services get a description.
- Shop settings (docs/api/phase11-shop-settings.md), the same panels in both workspaces: location and contact (district, street, email, map pin with "use my location"), opening hours to the quarter hour with closed dates, and a shop policy: receipt header and footer, VAT rate and number (receipts show the VAT inside the total), which payment methods the counter takes, and social links. Product shops also edit delivery zones in full (add, rename, districts, fee) and their return window; service providers set the start-time interval, how far ahead clients book, reminder timing and tip choices. The register and service checkout only offer accepted methods (the API refuses others), receipts print the shop's text and the actual cashier, and the provider calendar spans the shop's real hours.
- Shop teams (docs/api/phase10-shop-team.md): owners invite managers and staff by email; each person signs in with their own account. A new **Team** page in the product shop workspace and a **People and access** panel in the provider team page show who has access, change roles (owner only), give an existing calendar member a login, remove or restore access, and withdraw invites. Roles are enforced by the API: managers run the shop and invite staff but can't change the payout account or roles; staff see settings read-only.
- Staff security (docs/api/phase9-staff-security.md):
  - **Two-step sign-in (TOTP) for staff**: required before the console opens, checked again every 12 hours and on every new sign-in. Set-up shows a QR code and the key, then 10 one-time recovery codes. Keys are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM, `VBIZ_DATA_KEY`), codes can't be replayed, and 5 wrong codes sign the session out.
  - **Staff invites**: a super-admin invites by email with a role; the single-use link (7 days) lets a new person join with a 12+ character password, or an existing account confirm itself with its password.
  - **Settings → Staff**: your own two-step status and new recovery codes, the staff list (role, two-step, last active), changing roles, removing staff, resetting two-step sign-in for a lost phone (reason required, signs them out), and pending invites to withdraw.
  - **First super-admin**: a new environment creates it on start from `VBIZ_BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_*`, with a password that must be changed at first sign-in. No demo accounts outside the local profile.
  - The last active super-admin can't be demoted or suspended. Staff passwords need 12+ characters. Every step is in the audit log.
- Super-admin console:
  - **Settings**: commission %, payout delay and the riel exchange rate are database settings a super-admin changes from the console (other staff see them read-only). Saving asks first and the audit log records old → new values. They replace `vbiz.payment.commission-pct`, `vbiz.payouts.release-after` and the frontend's fixed 4,100 rate.
  - **Categories**: add, rename (English and Khmer), pick icon and colour, show or hide in the marketplace, reorder. The storefront reads names from the API, so a new category needs no deploy.
  - **Overview**: a 7 / 30 / 90-day switch kept in the URL, KPIs with sparklines and average order value, "New" or "N×" instead of huge percentages, queue cards that turn green when clear, a sales / orders chart with hover details, top shops with share bars, sales by category, growth, and a refresh every minute.
  - **Audit log**: grouped by day, with an icon and tone per action and settings changes shown as old → new.
  - Shop, user and order lists: each row's name is a real link (Cmd / Ctrl-click opens a new tab).
- Pages now follow the platform settings: the landing pricing card and fee calculator use the current commission; the payout timing on the landing page, seller settings and console Payouts page uses the current payout delay; every riel amount uses the current rate.
- Provider Services page redesign: search and category chips, an "Add to <category>" button per table (opens the form with it chosen), team avatars with names (or "No one assigned"), 30-day bookings with a bar, Online / Hidden next to the switch, a deal's discount under the price, inline actions, phone cards, and retail products as a shelf of cards with stock.

### Changed

- Shops run their own team; the console runs the platform. The console's shop page shows the owner and the team's size instead of every member's name and email, the shop activity log is no longer readable from the console, and the console's staff tab is named "Platform staff".
- Staff two-step sign-in is optional by default: the super-admin and other staff open the console with their password; anyone can still turn two-step sign-in on in Settings → Staff (then it is asked on every sign-in), and `VBIZ_STAFF_MFA_REQUIRED=true` makes it compulsory again. Locally, the demo staff accounts no longer get demo authenticator keys unless it is required.
- The Seller's settings page saves to the API (`PATCH /api/businesses/{id}`): online, auto-accept, prep time, low-stock default, pickup, COD, delivery fees, free-delivery threshold, KHQR merchant id and the shop details were only kept in the browser before.
- Adding to the cart while the account's server cart is still loading no longer loses the item (the change is merged into the server cart instead of being overwritten).
- A deal may have an empty description (the form allows it; the API refused it).

- An online booking or order only links the customer's account to a shop client found by phone when that phone is the account's own (typing someone else's number no longer takes over their client record).

- The shop code of Nita's Water Festival promotion is `NITAWATER` (`WATERFEST` is the platform's code).

- Removed the provider mocks (`lib/mock/provider.ts`, `provider-history.ts`) and the customer's local bookings (shop store v5); the remaining mock repo only holds the seller's POS, customers, promotions and reviews (Phase 5).
